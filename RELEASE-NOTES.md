# Nothing X for Mac 1.0.0

First public release of the unofficial Mac companion for Nothing and CMF audio devices.

- Resizable main window with 4:3 and square presets, plus a menu-bar dashboard for quick controls. Closing the main window leaves nearby pairing and the menu-bar app running.
- Catalog support for 23 audio models and 76 Fast Pair identifiers, including hardware and color variants.
- Device-specific battery, noise, equaliser, Bass Enhance, Dual Connection, low-latency, wear detection, and gesture controls where supported. Connecting reads your saved settings.
- Public build with system typography, device symbols, and original app artwork.

## Install

Open `Nothing-X-Mac-1.0.0-universal.dmg` and drag **Nothing X** to **Applications**. The build targets macOS 13 and later on Apple silicon and Intel. It is ad-hoc signed and has no Apple notarization. [Apple’s first-launch guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445)

## Compatibility

Ear (2024) and CMF Buds Pro 2 were confirmed on hardware during development. Other models still need live testing. CMF Speaker S and Speaker M have discovery and pairing support. Their companion controls, including Stereo and Party Mode, still need verification.
